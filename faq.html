FAQ

A clear starting point.

Answers to common questions about working with D.BROWN.

What services do you provide?+

We provide software development, programming, web development, IT consulting, website maintenance, technical support, and other digital technology services.

Who can use your services?+

Individuals, entrepreneurs, startups, businesses, and organizations requiring technology solutions.

Do you work with international clients?+

Yes. Depending on the project requirements, we may provide technology services to clients both within Nigeria and internationally.

How do I request a service?+

Share your requirements through our contact page. Once our business contact channels are available, you can send your enquiry directly.

How much does a project cost?+

Costs depend on the project scope, complexity, features, development time, and technical requirements. After reviewing your requirements, we can discuss an appropriate quotation.

Do you provide ongoing support?+

Yes. Depending on the project and agreement, ongoing technical support, updates, and maintenance may be available.

Can you build custom software?+

Yes. We develop custom software based on your requirements and intended functionality.

Can you maintain an existing website?+

Yes. We can review your website and provide maintenance, updates, troubleshooting, or improvements where appropriate.
